# Google Workspace Teardown: AI and the Future of Work

Live site: https://workspace-teardown.lovable.app/

Survey: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdUzezknP7WUrMNLvjMgGRqAHuoth4JHbApNPA3dEgNc6HGBA/viewform

An independent product teardown of AI in Google Workspace, built by Hassan Ahmad as a product manager candidate. I am not affiliated with Google.

## What I did

- Audited 12 AI features across Gmail, Meet and Docs.
- Benchmarked Workspace against Microsoft 365 Copilot and Notion.
- Ran a pilot user survey with 12 respondents (13 Oct 2026).
- Turned the findings into 3 product bets with a roadmap and success metrics.

## Key findings

Trust comes first: 12 of 12 respondents verify AI output at least sometimes, and 9 of 12 always do. Finding information spread across tools is the next biggest pain, raised by 6 of 12. Respondents report losing about 1.1 hours a week, self reported.

## The 3 bets

1. Ask Workspace: one question box that answers from a user's mail, files and calendar with linked sources and admin controls, also reaching non Google sources through connectors.
2. An assistant that acts: meeting notes to tracked to dos and scheduling, with approval before acting.
3. A trust, control and discovery layer: citations, clear data handling indicators and plain language prompt help.

## Limits

- The sample is small and non random, recruited from my personal network, so findings are directional.
- 6 of 12 respondents are IT professionals, 10 of 12 use Microsoft 365 weekly and 4 of 12 use no Workspace app, so findings describe cross app and AI behavior in general, not only Workspace users.
- Responses and hours lost are self reported, and two pain themes were coded by me from open ended answers.
- Competitive ratings and feature observations are my own and should be checked against the current products.
- I am still collecting responses, especially from Workspace heavy users, teachers, homemakers and IT admins. The survey takes about 5 minutes.
