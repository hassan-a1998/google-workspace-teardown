# Google Workspace Teardown: AI and the Future of Work

Live site: https://workspace-teardown.lovable.app/

An independent product teardown of AI in Google Workspace, built by Hassan Ahmad as a product manager candidate. I am not affiliated with Google.

## What I did

- Audited 12 AI features across Gmail, Meet and Docs.
- Benchmarked Workspace against Microsoft 365 Copilot and Notion.
- Ran a pilot user survey on how people use AI at work (5 respondents, so findings are directional).
- Turned the findings into 3 product bets with a roadmap and success metrics.

## Key finding

The opportunity is not more AI features. It is connecting information across apps with answers people can verify. Every respondent checks AI output, and finding information across apps was a top pain point. My lead bet is Ask Workspace, one question box that answers from a user's mail, files and calendar with linked sources and admin controls.

## Where I was wrong

My data contradicted two of my starting hypotheses. I kept them on the site on purpose, because a teardown should show what the evidence did not support.

## Limits

- The pilot survey is small and drawn from my own network, so it is directional, not statistically representative.
- Competitive ratings are my own observations from public product pages and announcements, and Workspace features change often.
- I am expanding the survey to 15 to 20 respondents.
